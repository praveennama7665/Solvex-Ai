Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions that are frequently asked

What is Solvex-AI?
Solvex-AI ek platform hai jo Poornima University ke students ko real industry problems solve karne ka mauka deta hai. Students team banate hain, faculty guide karte hain, companies problems deti hain.
How can students join?
Signup page pe jao, apni Poornima email use karo (jaise 2022cs001@poornima.edu.in), Student role select karo aur account banao. Phir login karke problems browse karo.
How to post an industry problem?
Provider account banao company email se, login karo, Provider Dashboard se "Post Problem" click karo. Faculty approve karti hai phir students dekh paenge.
When will I receive the certificate?
Project submit karne ke baad faculty review karti hai. Approve hone par automatically certificate generate hota hai jo PDF mein download kar sakte ho.
What is the role of faculty?
Faculty problems approve/reject karte hain, student projects review karte hain aur certificates issue karte hain. Poornima email hona chahiye.
Is the platform completely free?
Haan! Students aur Faculty ke liye Solvex-AI bilkul free hai. Industry providers ke liye bhi abhi free hai.
