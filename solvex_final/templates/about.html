Our Mission

What is Solvex-AI?

Solvex-AI is a Real-World Problem Solver Platform that bridges the gap between industries and university students. Industries face real challenges — students have creativity and innovation power.

We bring them together so students can work on real problems, gain professional experience, and deliver impactful solutions while earning verified certificates.

Industry-Posted Problems

Real companies post their challenges on our platform for students to solve.

Faculty Supervision

Every problem is reviewed and approved by qualified faculty before assignment.

Verified Certificates

Students earn industry-recognized certificates on project completion.

Problem Discovery

Browse approved real-world industry problems and choose what excites you.

Team Building

Form teams with classmates, assign roles, and collaborate on solutions.

Build & Submit

Develop your solution, upload progress, and submit to faculty for review.

Get Certified

Receive your digital certificate and industry recognition upon completion.

Process

How It Works

A simple 4-step journey from problem to certified solution

1

Industry Posts Problem

Companies submit real challenges they need solved — from software to automation.

2

Faculty Approves

Faculty reviews and approves problems to ensure they are appropriate and achievable.

3

Students Build

Students form teams, accept problems, and build their solutions with faculty guidance.

4

Certificate Earned

Completed solutions are verified and students receive industry-recognized certificates.
