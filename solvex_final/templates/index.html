About Solvex-Ai Platform

Solvex-Ai (Real-World Problem Solver Platform) is an initiative of Poornima University designed to connect real industry problems with talented students and experienced faculty members.

The platform allows organizations, startups and businesses to submit real Coding and IT challenges. These problems are reviewed by faculty members and then solved by skilled students, creating practical exposure and real-world learning opportunities.

How Solvex-Ai Works

1. Industry Posts Problem

Organizations submit real-world Coding or IT problems including project requirements and timelines.

2. Faculty Review

Faculty members analyze and approve problems to ensure quality, safety and academic value.

3. Students Build Solutions

Skilled students work on approved challenges and develop practical solutions under faculty mentorship.

Who Can Use Solvex-Ai Platform

Solvex-Ai connects industry professionals, university faculty, and talented students on a single platform to solve real-world Coding and IT challenges.

Problem Providers

Businesses, startups, NGOs, and organizations can submit real-world Coding or IT challenges that need innovative solutions.

Every submitted problem is reviewed by faculty before being assigned to student teams.

Faculty Mentors

Faculty members review submitted problems and guide students throughout the development process.

Their mentorship ensures quality solutions, structured learning, and academic credibility.

University Students

Students collaborate in teams to develop practical solutions for real industry problems.

This helps students gain real-world experience, technical skills, and verified project certificates.
